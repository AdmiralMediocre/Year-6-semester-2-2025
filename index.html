Multiple Choice Question Preparation

How to Use This Repository

  1. Select one of the MCQ modules from the grid below to open the quiz.
  2. Read each question carefully and click on the response box you believe is correct.
  3. The system will immediately highlight your selection in green for correct or red for wrong.
  4. Follow the progress bar at the top of the screen to track your completion.
  5. Check your final results on the score bar at the end of the test.
